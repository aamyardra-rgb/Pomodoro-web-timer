
🍅 POMODORO WEB TIMER

A cute and interactive Pomodoro Timer designed to make my study sessions more focused, organized, and enjoyable! 📚✨


🌟 About the Project

Pomodoro Web Timer is a simple productivity tool inspired by the famous Pomodoro Technique 🍅. It helps users maintain a healthy study routine by 
dividing work into focused study sessions and short breaks.Whether you're preparing for exams, learning new skills, or working on personal projects,
this timer keeps you on track while providing a pleasant and cozy user experience. 🌸💻


✨ Features

✅ 25-Minute Focus Session 📖
Stay concentrated on your tasks without distractions.
✅ 5-Minute Break Session ☕🌿
Take short breaks to refresh your mind before the next study cycle.
✅ Pause & Resume Functionality ⏸️▶️
Pause the timer whenever needed and continue from where you left off.
✅ Light & Dark Mode Toggle 🌞🌙
Switch between light and dark themes according to your preference.
✅ Interactive & Cute User Interface 💕
Designed with a cozy and engaging look to make studying feel more enjoyable.
✅ Responsive Design 📱💻
Works smoothly across different screen sizes and devices.


🎯 How It Works
Click Start to begin a 25-minute study session.
Stay focused and complete your task. 
After the session ends, enjoy a 5-minute break. 
Repeat the cycle to maximize productivity and avoid burnout. 🌟


🛠️ Technologies Used
HTML5 🌐
CSS3 🎨
JavaScript ⚡
